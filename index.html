Dr. Pride Duve

I like to write equations for mosquitoes, birds and viruses and see what they predict 🦟📈🌍

2022 - present

PhD in Ecological Modelling from the University of Hamburg, completed in 2026 and supervised by Renke Lühken and Felix Sauer. I am a mathematical modeller and researcher at the Bernhard Nocht Institute for Tropical Medicine in Hamburg, Germany, in the Arbovirus Ecology group.

Most of my time goes into West Nile virus in Germany. I study how temperature, migratory and resident birds and the biology of Culex mosquitoes combine to decide where and when the virus shows up, and what we can realistically do about it. Our spatial model reproduces the known outbreak areas in eastern Germany and points to a recurring corridor of spread that runs from the eastern states through the north and on to the southwest. West Nile cases in Germany do not occur at random.

2021 - 2022

Master's in mathematics at the University of Cape Town, with a scholarship from AIMS South Africa that made the work possible. This is where I started modelling malaria. I extended a human and mosquito transmission model with childhood vaccination and an influx of infected immigrants, then used optimal control to compare vaccination, personal protection and treatment.

pet projects

Zero West Nile virus is a free interactive web app that goes with our control paper. Pick a climate scenario, then mix and match interventions (removing mosquito breeding sites, vaccinating horses, people adopting personal protection) and watch what happens to the season. One finding you can replay yourself is that mosquito control which starts in early spring and ramps up in early May can flatten the August peak in infected birds. Built with R Shiny and hosted at BNITM.
